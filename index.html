About Me

Welcome! I'm Will Mungas, a recent graduate in Computer Science at North Carolina State University.

I got into computers when I was younger because I wanted to make games, and because I loved to understand how things work 'under the hood'.

Learning to program and developing an understanding of 3d graphics has been like a huge behind-the-scenes movie special. Now when I look at software I can see past the smoke and mirrors and appreciate the cleverness behind features, effects, and design choices. I can also learn from these things to do similar
